Vhour Invasion

A Co-Op mode for Magic the Gathering

The gamemode revolves around players removing Invasion permanents from the board and decreasing the counter on the plane until they win.

The Vhour Invasion - Plane - The Vhour

At the beginning of the game, place 20 Invasion counters per player on The Vhour
Invasion. Whenever a permanent with Invasion leaves the battlefield, remove X Invasion counters from The Vhour Invasion, where X is that permanent’s converted Invasion cost.
When there are no counters in The Vhour Invasion, the players win the game.
Whenever any player has his life total reduced to 0, all players lose the game.

Players construct a commander deck but replace 40 cards with the Invasion cards.

Invasion

The gamemode revolves around removing cards with Invasion from the battlefield.
These cards are casted automatically during the upkeep of players and attack or damage the players in various ways. The players need to block or use other means to remove them.

Invasion

Cannot be discarded. At the start of your upkeep, you must cast a card with Invasion from your hand, if possible.
If it is a creature: Attacks each turn if possible. Cannot block or be blocked by other creatures with Invasion.

Other

The players can strategize and even specialize on their decks.
Maybe one player focus more on tanking attacks, and another focus more on healing, and another on dealing direct damage to the Invasion creatures or removing permanents.
